# Logo clarity restorations · 24 October 2026

The built-in imagegen tool restored these low-resolution raster marks for the experience cards. They are clarity restorations, not new official source artwork. Original files remain in `public/images/logos/`.

- `public/images/logos/btps-hd.webp`: based on the 438 × 170 official [Bukit Timah Primary School website wordmark](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/942/75cdbb48-4719-4efe-93a8-6bbc32d993cd/btps_website_logo.png).
- `public/images/logos/sst-football-hd.webp`: based on Ian’s supplied 150 × 150 SST Football logo.

Both outputs were visually checked for lettering, colours and identity, then encoded as lossless WebP without resizing. Existing source logos remain available for comparison.
